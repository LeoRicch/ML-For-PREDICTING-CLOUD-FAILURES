First time here? A quick guide
  1. Choose the activity level. Normal activity is a gentle starting point.
  2. Select Start simulation. The app creates pretend jobs and sends them to virtual servers.
  3. Watch the jobs and warnings. A warning means a job may fail in the next five simulated minutes. It can still succeed. Failed jobs wait in a retry queue and can recover when a server has room.

A server is a computer handling jobs, called tasks. CPU means computing power; memory means working space. Everything here is simulated—no files are actually processed. With retries enabled, new arrivals stop after the chosen workload duration, then pending jobs get up to 120 extra simulated minutes to finish.

3. Read warnings and outcomes

A warning is an estimate, not a guarantee.

A confirmed warning precedes an initial-attempt failure by 1–5 simulated minutes. A later recovery does not erase that failure. “False alarm” means the warning window passed without that failure. Repeated warnings are grouped.

Warnings will appear here when a trained model flags a task.
